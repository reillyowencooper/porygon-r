Details

Ratings are computed using Glicko-2 (Glickman 2012), with one rating period per tournament and the system constant τ = 0.5. Every player starts at a ranking of 1500 with rating deviation 350 and volatility 0.06. These constants could change in the future, but are the recommended starting defaults.

Player eligibility: at least 35 rounds played and final rating deviation (RD) ≤ 100. RD is the system's confidence in the rating; lower means more games and more certainty. Players below the eligibility cutoffs are excluded because their rating hasn't converged enough to compare meaningfully against established players. I chose 35 rounds to retain players who have theoretically completed 4 Phase 1s at majors. Banned players are excluded from the ranking system.

Click any player to see their rating trajectory over time, including the rating-deviation confidence band and a breakdown of every tournament they've played.

I plan to update this after every major tournament. Please submit issues or ideas on Github. I hope this is a fun and useful resource for competitive players!
